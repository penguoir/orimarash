I'm Ori Marash. I like working on high-impact projects. I graduated with first-class honours from the University of Bath with a BSc in Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence.

I'm working on [FrameSports.AI](https://www.framesports.ai/) where we use modern computer vision approaches to analyze rugby games. Before that, I was the lead developer at [DitchCarbon](https://ditchcarbon.com/), which used machine learning to gather hundreds of thousands of company environmental reports and provide the carbon data as an API to procurement companies [such as SAP](https://store.sap.com/dcp/en/product/display-2002000119_live_v1/ditchcarbon-product-carbon-footprint-data).
