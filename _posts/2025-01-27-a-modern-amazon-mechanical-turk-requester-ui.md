At FrameSports we needed some data entry done quickly, so we turned to Amazon's Mechanical Turk. I wanted to check that our hundreds of tasks were going through the system properly and that the system at large worked as I expected. But I couldn't find a user interface that let me do that, so I build my own.

Send me an email if you want access to this:

-   First, you add your MTurk AWS credentials to the system. You can manage multiple MTurk accounts and sandbox accounts.
-   You can then view all your HITs, their assignments, statuses, etc.
